# MNCS Contribution Attestation — Concept Draft v0.1

> **Non-binding research draft.** This is not a contributor license agreement and is not currently a required legal certification.

The goal is to supplement, not rewrite, established contribution mechanisms such as the Developer Certificate of Origin.

A future implementation could ask a submitter to attest, to the best of their knowledge, that:

1. they have authority to submit the contribution under the project's stated terms;
2. known material third-party sources have been disclosed or referenced;
3. machine involvement is described using the project's provenance classification rather than hidden or falsely attributed;
4. they are not representing that they personally authored every expressive element merely because they submitted machine-generated material;
5. unresolved rights or provenance questions have been marked as unresolved rather than converted into unsupported assertions;
6. evidence records supplied with the contribution have not knowingly been falsified.

## Relationship to the DCO

If adopted, the preferred approach is:

`DCO 1.1 (unchanged) + MNCS provenance attestation`

The DCO text should not be modified and still governs the contributor's conventional right-to-submit certification. The MNCS supplement would describe machine-native provenance and uncertainty.

Qualified counsel should review any binding version before project adoption.
